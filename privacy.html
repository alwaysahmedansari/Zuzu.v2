Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026.

1. Who we are

ZUZU is a reading and writing community operated by Ahmed Ansari of Lahore, Pakistan ("we"). This policy explains what personal data we handle and why.

2. Data we collect

Account data: your email address and, if you use Google sign-in, your name. Content you create: stories, comments, applause and saved items. Support data: the name, email and message you send through our contact form. Technical data: our hosting and database providers may log IP address and device information for security and operation.

3. How we use it

To run ZUZU, show your public content with your display name, answer support messages and keep the service safe. We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

4. Service providers

We use Supabase (sign-in and database), Google (only if you choose Google sign-in) and GitHub Pages (hosting). Cover images are loaded directly from the link a writer provides, so the website hosting that image can see the visitor's IP address.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage only to keep you signed in and remember your theme. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

6. Your choices

You can delete your own stories and comments. To access, correct or delete your account and data, contact us through Contact & Support.

7. Retention and security

We keep data while your account is active or as needed to run the service. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service is completely secure.

8. Children

ZUZU is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
