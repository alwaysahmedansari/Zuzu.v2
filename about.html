About ZUZU

A world of words

What is ZUZU?

ZUZU is a friendly home for stories, essays and honest reflections, written in English and Urdu. Anyone can read for free. Sign in to write, applaud, comment and save what you love.

Why we built it

Good writing deserves a calm, colourful place that feels human. No noise, no endless feeds of outrage, just words worth your time.

For writers

Publish in minutes. Add a cover by pasting a link to an image you have the right to use (3:2 images look best) and ZUZU displays it for you. You always own what you write.

Our principles

Respect readers and writers. Credit original work. Keep ZUZU welcoming to everyone.

Get in touch

Questions, complaints or ideas? Use Contact & Support and our team will read your message.
